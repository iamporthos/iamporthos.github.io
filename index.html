Springy

Seats two in the footprint of one stool, then stacks away when it isn't needed — built from off-the-shelf metal rod and ratchet straps, so the whole thing is sourceable without custom tooling.

And the capacity is verified, not claimed: I'm over 300 lbs, and I sat on it. It held.
